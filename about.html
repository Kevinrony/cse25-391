Our Story

Pioneering Sustainable Waste Management Since 2010

EcoWaste Solutions was founded with a simple but powerful vision: to transform how Botswana handles its waste. What started as a small residential collection service in Gaborone has grown into a comprehensive waste management company serving thousands of homes and businesses nationwide.

We believe waste is a resource, not a problem. With the right systems, processes and mindset, we can turn what most people throw away into something valuable – energy, compost, recycled materials, and a cleaner environment.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, eco-conscious waste management services that reduce landfill waste, promote recycling and contribute to a sustainable environment.

Our Vision

A zero-waste Botswana where every citizen, household and business plays an active role in protecting the planet for future generations.
